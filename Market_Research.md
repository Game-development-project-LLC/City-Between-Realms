## 4) Unique Selling Proposition (USP)
- **Cross‑realm systems**: Player learns **rules that differ per realm** (time flow, access, economy, tech/magic) and exploits them across a **shared city map**.
- **Readable rules UI** and a **compact, revisitable hub** avoid bloat; puzzles teach systems progressively.
- **Personal mystery arc** connecting the city’s nightly “slide” to the protagonist’s story.

**Why this matters to our audience:**  
- Players in this niche seek **“smart” puzzles** and **narrative cohesion** over raw scope; the cross‑realm design reinforces both.
